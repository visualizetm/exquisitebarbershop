REPORT 13f: THE LOCKED FRONT DESK TABLET, AND TAKINGS AFTER THE DISCOUNT
Summary: A front desk tablet now pairs with a one-use code from Settings, Check in tablets and runs the locked page at /checkin on its own auth level: it can walk clients in, check them in and show who is in, and reaches nothing else (every owner and team route answers it 403).
